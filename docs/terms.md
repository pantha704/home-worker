## Allowed use

- Convert documents you have the right to process into reviewable notes.
- Print those notes after you have reviewed every page.
- Use the bundled SIL-OFL handwriting *personas* (generic licensed fonts), not anyone's real handwriting.

## Forbidden use

- Forgery, impersonation, or reproducing a specific person's handwriting or signature.
